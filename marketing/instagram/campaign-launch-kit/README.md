# Try Instant Fit — Instagram Campaign Launch Kit

This kit adds two new Google Flow / Gemini Omni Flash-ready Reels to the original
`video-01-google-flow-package`.

## Campaign order

1. **What It Does** — use `video-01-google-flow-package`
2. **In-Store QR** — use `REEL-02-BOUTIQUE-QR-PROMPT.txt`
3. **Global Western Suit** — use `REEL-03-WESTERN-SUIT-PROMPT.txt`

## Universal settings

- Vertical 9:16
- Highest available resolution; final target 1080 × 1920
- 30 fps
- 10–15 seconds
- Clean premium fashion-technology style
- Captions must remain readable without sound
- One CTA only: `DM “DEMO”`

## Universal privacy rule

Every face must remain strongly blurred for every frame. This includes generated
subjects, authentic phone screens, results, thumbnails, reflections, mirrors, and cover
images. Never reconstruct, sharpen, enhance, or reveal a face. Reject any generation
where facial blur weakens for even one frame.

## Real product rule

Use `real-phone-flow/flow-1.jpg` through `flow-11.jpg` in numeric order. Do not ask AI
to redraw or reinterpret these screens. Keep the real Try Instant Fit logo unchanged.
