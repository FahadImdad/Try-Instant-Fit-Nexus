# In-store QR Reel

This package contains the complete 11-frame in-store QR video.

The real Try Instant Fit UI from the supplied website flow is composited into frames 03–08. It has not been regenerated or replaced.

Upload the frames in numerical order, then use `VIDEO-PROMPT.md`.

Recommended output: 9:16, 1080 × 1920, 15 seconds, 30 fps.
