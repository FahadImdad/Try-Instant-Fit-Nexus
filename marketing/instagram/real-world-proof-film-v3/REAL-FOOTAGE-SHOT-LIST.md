# Optional Real Footage — Record on Any Phone

Record vertically at 1080p/30 fps. Use daylight or normal shop lighting. Do not use
cinematic mode, filters, artificial depth blur, or dramatic color grading.

## Shot 1 — Choose the garment

- Duration: 2 seconds
- Frame only hands and torso; keep the face outside the frame
- A shopper lifts one modest garment slightly from a boutique rack
- Keep real shop sounds

## Shot 2 — Open Try Instant Fit

- Duration: 2 seconds
- Over-the-shoulder phone shot
- Open the real product link or scan the real QR
- Ensure no name, email, phone number, or notification is visible
- If any face appears on the phone, blur it

## Shot 3 — See the result

- Duration: 2 seconds
- Close-up of the real result screen
- One natural thumb tap or scroll
- Keep the screen readable
- Blur every face shown on the phone

Use a tripod or rest the phone against a stable object. Slight real handheld movement is
acceptable; artificial camera shake is not.
