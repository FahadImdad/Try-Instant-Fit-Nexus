# Try Instant Fit — Real-World Proof Film V3

This version is intentionally believable. It avoids generated stores, holograms,
fantasy lighting, synthetic people, and dramatic AI camera moves.

## Creative rule

If a viewer could not realistically record the shot with a phone or camera today, do
not use it.

## Asset policy

- Model and result images are genuine supplied Try Instant Fit assets.
- Phone frames are from the live Try Instant Fit product.
- Logo is the authentic Try Instant Fit logo.
- Google Omni should add only subtle, physically plausible motion.
- Do not generate new people, clothing, stores, UI, or results.

## Produce

1. Generate the two short before/after clips separately.
2. Record the three optional real-life phone shots in `REAL-FOOTAGE-SHOT-LIST.md`.
3. Assemble everything using `MASTER-PROMPT.txt`.

If real footage is not available, use the authentic phone frames instead. Do not replace
missing footage with an invented AI boutique scene.
